## Infrastructure repair disclosure (evaluator, not gate logic)

The first v3.2 run BLOCKED at 26/55 with "prompt identity pin missing or mismatched" on every
honest pin. Root cause: the evaluator's trace reader `_trace_prompt_user` looked for the prompt
*nested inside* the `participant_invoked` event, but the trace convention — proven by both
writers, the runner (`runners/w04-model-runner.py` line 210) and the controls builder
(`evals/w04_r3_controls.py` line 238), and by the historical byte-identity guards — records the
prompt as its own `participant_prompt` event. The helper therefore returned `None` for every
record, and the v3.2 hard pin check could never verify any pin: a broken gate, not a lenient one.
Fix: the reader now scans for `participant_prompt` events. Direction is strict-compatible — no
check was weakened or removed; the fix makes the existing hard check able to pass an honest pin
and fail a dishonest one (W04/W05 negatives still BLOCK). Evaluator sha before the fix:
`61298b75b6ab0a5b3b348b78a8e38519d4094e33e845bf39b41a7060fc027436`; after:
`b5c22f915a107900596b7f4e37c99acc4250e08e6278d77ed7898869cb96e1ca`. No participant, fixture,
workflow, or historical record was modified.
