# W11 creative production (MISSION)

Objective: deliver a finished creative artifact through revision under degrading state.

Mission-depth requirements (all five, else demoted to COMPOSITE):

1. Persistent state carried across sessions (brief, assets, revision history survive restart).
2. At least one mid-run interruption the runner must recover from (lost session, revoked tool, failed render).
3. At least one dependency change after work started (asset swap, brief revision, tool replacement).
4. At least two parallel tracks coordinated (e.g. visual + audio, edit + review).
5. Extended execution where unmanaged state degrades (stale previews, drifted assets, conflicting revisions).

Evidence: final artifact + revision trace + evaluator checks. Video required (inspectable, never proof alone).
